
QUESTIONS:

Q1. Staff use an unapproved public chatbot to summarise confidential contracts. What is this an example of?
    (A) Data sovereignty
    (B) Shadow AI
    (C) Differential privacy
    (D) The NIST AI Risk Management Framework

Q2. Which role is responsible for independently verifying that AI systems meet policy and compliance requirements?
    (A) Data engineer
    (B) AI auditor
    (C) Platform engineer
    (D) Machine learning engineer

Q3. What does differential privacy achieve?
    (A) It adds statistical noise so no individual record can be singled out from aggregate results
    (B) It encrypts data while it is being processed in memory
    (C) It removes all data from a training set entirely
    (D) It is a synonym for data masking

Q4. Which regulatory framework classifies AI systems by risk level and prohibits some AI uses outright?
    (A) NIST AI Risk Management Framework
    (B) ISO AI standards
    (C) EU AI Act
    (D) OECD standards

Q5. What is the distinction between a sanctioned and an unsanctioned AI tool?
    (A) Sanctioned tools have gone through formal organisational approval; unsanctioned tools have not
    (B) Sanctioned tools are always more accurate
    (C) Unsanctioned tools are always open source
    (D) There is no meaningful distinction between the two



ANSWER KEY (For Instructor Use)
==================================================
Q1: (B) Shadow AI
Q2: (B) AI auditor
Q3: (A) It adds statistical noise so no individual record can be singled out from aggregate results
Q4: (C) EU AI Act
Q5: (A) Sanctioned tools have gone through formal organisational approval; unsanctioned tools have not
