The Takeaway

Four habits that keep AI-assisted work safe to send.

  • 01AI accelerates. You own the output.Every draft passes through your judgement before it is sent, published or filed. Nothing goes out unchanged.
  • 02Match the task to the stakes.Legal, medical, financial, safety-critical, HR and people decisions, and anything published externally need a human to lead.
  • 03A summary is a way in, not a substitute.What it left out is not flagged. For anything consequential, read the original.
  • 04Facts, dates, names, citations.Checked before the work leaves your desk. "The AI produced it" is not a defence for an error that reaches someone else.
