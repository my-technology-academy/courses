The Takeaway

Four habits that hold whatever tool you are using.

  • 01When in doubt, leave it out.Personal data, employee records, credentials, commercially sensitive material and confidential documents do not go into a public or unapproved tool.
  • 02Policy governs, not the tool's defaults.Retention and residency vary by plan and are set by the vendor. Your organisation's policy decides what you may use and share — and a policy gap is not permission.
  • 03Verify through a channel you already trust.Fluent writing and a familiar voice prove nothing now. Urgency is a warning sign, not a reason to skip the check — and never verify using the details in the message itself.
  • 04Keep oversight of what the tool does for you.Content you feed in can carry instructions of its own. Review what comes back, and never let a tool take an irreversible action unreviewed.
