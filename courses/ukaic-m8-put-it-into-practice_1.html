Stage 04 — CompleteModule 8 of 8

The Takeaway

Four judgements that decide whether you can stand behind the work.

  • 01AI is not neutral by default.Bias enters from the training data, from the design choices, and from the context a system is deployed into. Where AI touches a decision about people, checking for it is part of the job.
  • 02Transparency, accountability and explainability are three obligations, not one.Say when AI was involved. Own the outcome — “the AI did it” is never a defence. And be able to justify any decision it helped you make.
  • 03AI proposes. A person decides. A person owns the outcome.Oversight is not bureaucracy — it is the condition that makes AI use legitimate where the stakes are real.
  • 04Place the task before you start it.Use · Use with verification · Escalate · Do not use. When you cannot confidently place something in the first two, move one zone more cautious and ask.
Before you close this — your charter
Five lines, in your own words, written wherever you will actually see them again. There are no right answers here; the writing is the point.
  1. When and how I will say that AI was involved.
  2. What I will never put through an AI tool.
  3. How I will verify AI-generated content before I use it.
  4. What counts as genuine human review for my consequential work.
  5. What I will do when I am not sure whether AI use is appropriate.
AI can assist. Humans remain accountable.
That is the last thing this programme has to say, and the one thing that does not change as the tools do.
