UKAIC: Module 4

4.1 THE TCFC FRAMEWORK
Task — what to do
Context — background needed
Format — what it looks like
Constraints — rules/limits
4.2 CLEAR INSTRUCTIONS
Be specific about the outcome · remove ambiguous words ("good", "professional") · state it directly
4.3 DIAGNOSING WEAK PROMPTS
Check for: missing context · no format · bundled asks · no constraints
4.4 ITERATIVE REFINEMENT
Treat the first response as a draft. Give a specific, targeted follow-up — not "make it better."
4.5 EXAMPLES & PERSONAS
An example anchors style faster than describing it. A persona guides tone/framing — not new facts.
WORKED EXAMPLE
"Write a two-paragraph summary of the attached customer feedback report [Task] for a director who hasn't read the full report and cares about churn risk [Context]. Use plain prose with no headings [Format], and keep it under 150 words, avoiding jargon [Constraints]."
EXAM TRAPS
⚠️ A longer prompt is always better — clarity and specificity matter more than length
⚠️ Needing a follow-up means the first prompt failed — iteration is normal practice
⚠️ A persona gives the model new expertise — it changes tone and framing only
