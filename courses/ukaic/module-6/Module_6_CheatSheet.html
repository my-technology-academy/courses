UKAIC: Module 6

6.1 HALLUCINATION
Fluent, confident output that is factually wrong or invented. Predicting ≠ retrieving facts.
6.2 OVERCONFIDENCE
A model's tone carries no information about accuracy. It doesn't "know" when it's wrong.
6.3 RELIABILITY
Knowledge cut-offs + thin/outdated training data both reduce reliability, fluently.
6.4 VERIFICATION TECHNIQUES
1. Independent source
2. Ask for the source
3. Cross-check a 2nd way
4. Apply domain knowledge
6.5 HUMAN-IN-THE-LOOP
A person reviews AI output at a defined point before it takes effect — consistently, not just at the start.
6.6 ALWAYS VERIFY
Facts & figures · legal · medical · financial · code-related output
EXAM TRAPS
⚠️ A confident tone means an answer is accurate — confidence and accuracy are unrelated
⚠️ Hallucination only happens on obscure questions — it can happen anywhere fluency and truth diverge
⚠️ Verification is only for "important" documents — stakes and data sensitivity decide, not format
