
QUESTIONS:

Q1. What is AI hallucination?
    (A) When an AI tool refuses to answer a question
    (B) Fluent, confident-sounding output that is factually incorrect or invented
    (C) When an AI tool exceeds its context window
    (D) When an AI tool takes too long to respond

Q2. An AI tool states a fabricated fact in the same confident tone as a correct one. What does this show?
    (A) The model always flags uncertain answers
    (B) A model's tone of confidence carries no information about accuracy
    (C) The model is deliberately lying
    (D) The model has run out of tokens

Q3. What does "human-in-the-loop" mean?
    (A) AI output is never used without being rewritten entirely
    (B) A person reviews or approves AI output at a defined point before it takes effect
    (C) A human trains the AI model directly
    (D) AI tools require a licensed human operator to run at all

Q4. What should determine how much verification effort a piece of AI output gets?
    (A) How long the AI took to respond
    (B) The stakes of being wrong and the sensitivity of what's involved
    (C) Whether the output is long or short
    (D) Whether the AI tool is free or paid

Q5. An AI tool states a fabricated statistic in exactly the same confident tone as a correct one nearby. What does this best illustrate?
    (A) The model has exceeded its context window
    (B) A model's confident tone carries no information about accuracy
    (C) The model's temperature is set too low
    (D) The model is using retrieval-augmented generation
