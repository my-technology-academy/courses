UKAIC: Module 8

8.1 BIAS & FAIRNESS
Systematic unfair skew in output. Usually enters through training data that under-represents groups or reflects historical inequality.
8.2 THREE RELATED TERMS
Transparency — open that AI is involved
Disclosure — telling those affected
Explainability — saying roughly why
8.3 ACCOUNTABILITY
Humans remain responsible for AI-assisted decisions. "The AI said so" is never a valid justification.
8.4 POLICY & GOVERNANCE
Acceptable-use rules + oversight structure that turns principles into practice.
8.5 REGULATION & STANDARDS
Still emerging and evolving worldwide. No specific law is examinable — awareness only.
8.6 RESPONSIBLE USE, DAILY
Keep a person in the decision · consider who could be affected unfairly · follow organisational policy
EXAM TRAPS
⚠️ "The AI decided" removes human responsibility — accountability always stays with people
⚠️ Disclosure and explainability are the same thing — they are distinct and related
⚠️ AI regulation is fixed and settled — it is actively emerging and evolving
