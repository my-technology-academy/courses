5.5 THREE QUESTIONS BEFORE YOU RELY ON AI OUTPUT
1. What happens if this is wrong?
2. Is the data sensitive or confidential?
3. Will a person check it before use?
GENERALLY A GOOD FIT
Drafting · summarising · planning structure · research orientation — reviewed before use
NEEDS REAL CAUTION
Final legal/medical/financial decisions · unchecked external use · confidential data, unapproved tool
5.6 HUMAN VERIFICATION
Checking AI output before relying on it is part of the normal workflow — not an optional extra or a sign something went wrong.
EXAM TRAPS
⚠️ An AI-drafted document is ready to send as-is — it's a first draft, still needs review
⚠️ AI research removes the need to check sources — it's a starting point only
⚠️ A "non-technical" task carries no risk — data sensitivity and error consequences matter more
