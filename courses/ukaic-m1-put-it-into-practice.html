The Takeaway

Carry these four judgements into any system you are asked to assess.

  • 01Automatic is not the same as intelligent.Fixed rules stay automation, however fast, digital or complex they are.
  • 02Judge the capability, not the label.AI performs prediction, classification, recognition or generation. “AI-powered” is a claim, not evidence.
  • 03Ask what the system learns from.If behaviour only changes when someone rewrites the rules, nothing is being learned from data.
  • 04“Not enough information” is a valid professional answer.When you are told what a system does but not how, ask before you decide.
