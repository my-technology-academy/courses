OSAI: Introduction to Red Teaming AI Systems

1.1 FOUNDATIONS
AI red teaming: adversarial assessment of AI systems to prove real impact before an attacker does
vs pentest: objective-driven & chains weaknesses, not scope-coverage
vs vuln assessment: red teaming exploits; assessment only enumerates
vs safety testing: security seeks system compromise (CIA); safety seeks harmful content
Text is code: any text the model reads may be an instruction
Chain to impact: an injection is a finding only when it leads to exfiltration or action
1.2 ATTACK SURFACE — 6 LAYERS
Model: guardrail bypass, jailbreaks, fingerprinting
Data/retrieval: RAG & training poisoning, indirect injection
Application/prompt: direct injection, output handling, prompt leakage
Agent/tool: tool abuse, confused deputy, MCP, A2A
Infrastructure: exposed serving, cloud identity, misconfig
Supply chain: malicious models, unsafe deserialization
1.3 FRAMEWORKS
OWASP LLM Top 10: names risks — LLM01 Injection, LLM04 Poisoning, LLM06 Excessive Agency, LLM07 Prompt Leakage
MITRE ATLAS: ATT&CK-style adversary tactics/techniques for AI → kill-chain narrative
NIST AI RMF: Govern · Map · Measure · Manage — Measure calls for red teaming
Rule of thumb: OWASP = risks · ATLAS = behaviour · NIST = governance

KEY DISTINCTIONS
Direct injectionIndirect injection
Payload typed into an input you controlPayload hidden in content the model later reads
Single-hop, immediateDeferred, multi-hop — basis of RAG/agent attacks
Jailbreak: subverts the model's content policy (safety lens)
Injection: subverts the app's control flow (security lens)
1.4 ENGAGEMENT METHODOLOGY
1 Scoping & RoE 2 Reconnaissance 3 Threat modeling 4 Exploitation 5 Post-exploitation & impact 6 Reporting Report each finding with: OWASP ID · ATLAS technique · evidence · impact · layered remediation
EXAM TRAPS & ACRONYMS
⚠️ Jailbreak ≠ prompt injection — content policy vs application control flow
⚠️ Indirect injection needs no access to the victim's prompt — it rides in via retrieved data
⚠️ A prompt injection alone is not a finding — it must be chained to demonstrated impact
⚠️ AI weaknesses are mitigated (layered, probabilistic), not "patched" like a deterministic bug
⚠️ The model is rarely the whole target — data, agent, and infra layers carry the impact
⚠️ OSAI weightings are not published — the exam is a 24-hour practical engagement
