The Takeaway

Four judgements to carry into any conversation about AI tools.

  • 01There is no single best AI tool.Generating, classifying, retrieving and acting are different capabilities. Match the category to the task.
  • 02Grounding is not a guarantee.RAG improves freshness and sourcing. It does not remove error, and it does not remove your review.
  • 03More autonomy needs more oversight, not less.An agent takes real actions across several steps, and a mistake early on carries forward into everything after it.
  • 04Capability, cost, data sensitivity, approval.Sensitivity is the one that can stop everything on its own — and approval never overrides it.
