The Takeaway

Four things to carry into every professional encounter with AI.

  • 01Fluent is not true.Hallucination comes from the prediction mechanism itself. Well-written output is not verified output — treat it as a capable but fallible first draft.
  • 02Looking like a source is not being a source.Statistics, citations, quotes and case studies are the four things models invent. Locate every one at its origin before you use it.
  • 03A second AI is not a check.Two models can reproduce the same error and agree with each other. Verification is against primary, non-AI sources — not a summary, not another model.
  • 04Ground it, flag it, cross-check it, own it.Legal, medical, financial, safety-critical, HR and people decisions, and anything published externally: six contexts where verification is not optional.
