
QUESTIONS:

Q1. An AI tool produces a first version of a policy email. What is the correct next step?
    (A) Send it immediately — it is already complete
    (B) A person reviews and edits it before it is used
    (C) Ask the AI to send it directly to the team
    (D) Discard it, since AI cannot help with drafting

Q2. Why should a summarisation prompt specify length and focus?
    (A) Otherwise the AI will refuse the request
    (B) Without it, the summary may treat every detail as equally important
    (C) It has no effect on the output
    (D) It changes the model's knowledge cut-off

Q3. What is the appropriate role of AI in research assistance?
    (A) A final, citable source
    (B) A starting point for orientation, not a substitute for checking sources
    (C) A replacement for all manual research
    (D) Only useful for numerical data

Q4. What matters most when judging whether a task is appropriate for AI assistance?
    (A) Whether the task sounds technical
    (B) The consequences of an error and the sensitivity of the data involved
    (C) How long the prompt is
    (D) Which AI tool category is used

Q5. A colleague wants to paste a customer's personal details into a public AI tool to help draft a reply. Using the checklist from this module, what is the main concern?
    (A) Drafting replies is not an appropriate use of AI
    (B) The task will take too long for AI to help with
    (C) It involves sensitive personal data in an unapproved tool
    (D) Public AI tools cannot draft replies



ANSWER KEY (For Instructor Use)
==================================================
Q1: (B) A person reviews and edits it before it is used
Q2: (B) Without it, the summary may treat every detail as equally important
Q3: (B) A starting point for orientation, not a substitute for checking sources
Q4: (B) The consequences of an error and the sensitivity of the data involved
Q5: (C) It involves sensitive personal data in an unapproved tool
