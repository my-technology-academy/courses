CAIA: AI Security, Privacy & Data

7.1 PROTECT: PERSONAL · CONFIDENTIAL · SENSITIVE · REGULATED
Only enter these into AI tools your organisation has approved — popularity ≠ approval.
7.2 DATA LEAKAGE
Confidential/sensitive info ending up where it shouldn't. Treat submission as one-way.
7.3 SHADOW AI
Unsanctioned tool use. Risk = lack of visibility, not bad intent.
7.4 AI-ENABLED THREATS
Deepfakes — fake media of real people
AI phishing — convincing scams at scale
Misinformation — credible-looking false content
7.5 PROMPT INJECTION
Hidden instructions inside content an AI tool reads (webpage, doc, email). Awareness only — not a user detection task.
7.6 THREE SAFE PRACTICES
Use approved tools · protect account access/credentials · follow data-classification and handling rules
EXAM TRAPS
⚠️ A well-known tool is automatically safe for confidential data — popularity ≠ approval
⚠️ Shadow AI implies malicious intent — usually well-meaning, unchecked use
⚠️ Prompt injection is something end users must detect technically — awareness is enough
