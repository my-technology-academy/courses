
QUESTIONS:

Q1. An unquoted service path exists on a Windows host that stores payroll data. Which term correctly describes the unquoted service path itself?
    (A) A risk
    (B) A threat
    (C) A vulnerability
    (D) An exploit

Q2. Which statement best describes the relationship between a threat and a vulnerability?
    (A) A threat is a weakness in a system; a vulnerability is the party who would abuse it
    (B) A threat exists independently of your systems; a vulnerability is a weakness within them
    (C) They are interchangeable terms for the same concept
    (D) A vulnerability only exists once a threat actor has been identified

Q3. A penetration test discovers that an application permits an unauthorised party to modify stored records. Which leg of the CIA triad is primarily affected?
    (A) Confidentiality
    (B) Integrity
    (C) Availability
    (D) None of the above

Q4. Why should a security control not depend on the secrecy of its design?
    (A) Because design details reliably leak, leaving the control with no strength once known
    (B) Because regulators prohibit undocumented controls
    (C) Because secrecy always reduces availability
    (D) Because obscurity has no legitimate use in any layered defence

Q5. Which instrument states auditable requirements that an organisation can be formally certified against?
    (A) A framework
    (B) A standard
    (C) A policy
    (D) A threat intelligence feed



ANSWER KEY (For Instructor Use)
==================================================
Q1: (C) A vulnerability
Q2: (B) A threat exists independently of your systems; a vulnerability is a weakness within them
Q3: (B) Integrity
Q4: (A) Because design details reliably leak, leaving the control with no strength once known
Q5: (B) A standard
==================================================
