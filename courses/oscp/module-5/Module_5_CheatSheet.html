OSCP: Information Gathering

5.1 THE PENTEST LIFECYCLE
Pre-engagement → Information gathering → Vuln analysis/exploitation → Post-exploitation/reporting
Info gathering recurs — every time the picture of the target changes
Passive vs active: does the target observe the action? That's the only test
5.2 PASSIVE INFORMATION GATHERING
Two approaches: third-party info about target / target's own public footprint via a third party
OSINT: organisational, people, technical, historical
Certificate transparency: every cert ever issued — exposes hidden subdomains
Passive DNS history: past IP resolutions, even post-migration
PORT SCANNING
nc -nvz -w1 <target> 1-1000
nmap -sC -sV -p- <target>
Filtered ≠ closed — filtered = undetermined, try another technique

LIVING OFF THE LAND & WORKFLOW
Using tools/binaries already on the target instead of introducing new ones — trusted by the OS, rarely flagged, nothing to upload. Recurs in Module 13 (AV evasion) and Module 15 (Windows enumeration).

EXAM TRAPS & ACRONYMS
⚠️ Passive means the target never observes the action — NOT "using few tools"
⚠️ A quiet/slow Nmap scan is still ACTIVE — any packet sent to the target counts
⚠️ Information gathering is not a one-time stage — it recurs across the whole lifecycle
⚠️ Filtered ≠ closed — filtered is undetermined, not confirmed absent
⚠️ Active DNS enumeration queries the target directly; passive DNS history reads third-party records
