OSCP: Penetration Testing with Kali Linux — General Course Introduction

1.1 WELCOME TO PWK
Course inventory: take stock of everything PEN-200 includes before starting
Attacking Kali VM: the learner's own Kali machine — the platform all work is performed from
PWK VPN: the connection established and interacted over to reach course environments
Module Exercises: per-module practice items; know how they are completed
Course name: PEN-200 = PWK (Penetration Testing with Kali). Syllabus version: PWK v3.0
1.2 HOW TO APPROACH THE COURSE
Learning model: conceptualised around increasing uncertainty — each stage gives less certainty than the last
Learning components: understand the different components PEN-200 delivers learning through
Why it matters: the model mirrors a real engagement, where no answer key exists
Deepened later: Module 3, Effective Learning Strategies, expands the theory behind this

EXAM TRAPS & ACRONYMS
⚠️ PEN-200 and PWK are the same course, not two courses — PWK is the historic short name
⚠️ PEN-200 is the course; OSCP is the certification earned from it. Do not use the names interchangeably
⚠️ The learning model is increasing uncertainty, not increasing difficulty — the variable is how much you are told, not how hard the target is
⚠️ Module 1 is orientation only; no offensive technique is taught until Module 5, Information Gathering
⚠️ Module order is fixed by the official syllabus — enumeration precedes exploitation, which precedes escalation
