
QUESTIONS:

Q1. A scan configured with valid domain credentials inspects a host's local patch level directly. What type of scan is this?
    (A) An unauthenticated scan
    (B) A lightweight NSE scan
    (C) An authenticated scan
    (D) A passive information gathering technique

Q2. What does a vulnerability scanner actually compare when producing a result?
    (A) Observed fingerprints against a maintained database of known weaknesses
    (B) The tester's notes against the client's expectations
    (C) One host's uptime against another's
    (D) The report's word count against a minimum threshold

Q3. Why must a raw vulnerability scanner result be manually verified before being reported as a finding?
    (A) Because scanner output can never be exported
    (B) Because scanner results include false positives and are candidates, not confirmed findings
    (C) Because clients never accept automated results under any circumstance
    (D) Because manual verification is required by law in every jurisdiction

Q4. What is a Nessus plugin?
    (A) A physical hardware dongle required to run Nessus
    (B) An individual, inspectable check within the scan engine that produced a specific result
    (C) A licence file needed only for authenticated scans
    (D) A category of NSE script

Q5. When is Nmap's NSE vuln scanning most appropriately used relative to a full Nessus scan?
    (A) As a complete replacement for Nessus in every engagement
    (B) For fast, lightweight checks or custom one-off conditions, alongside rather than instead of a dedicated scanner
    (C) Only when no ports are open on the target
    (D) Only during the reporting stage, after the engagement has ended



ANSWER KEY (For Instructor Use)
==================================================
Q1: (C) An authenticated scan
Q2: (A) Observed fingerprints against a maintained database of known weaknesses
Q3: (B) Because scanner results include false positives and are candidates, not confirmed findings
Q4: (B) An individual, inspectable check within the scan engine that produced a specific result
Q5: (B) For fast, lightweight checks or custom one-off conditions, alongside rather than instead of a dedicated scanner
