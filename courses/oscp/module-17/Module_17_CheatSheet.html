EXAM TRAPS & ACRONYMS
⚠️ Local (-L) reaches OUTWARD from the tester; Remote (-R) reaches BACK from the far end — opposite directions
⚠️ Dynamic ≠ always best — Local is simpler/clearer when the destination is already fixed and known
⚠️ Netsh is NOT encrypted like an SSH tunnel — configuration-level forwarding only
⚠️ Module 17 solves REACHABILITY; Module 18 solves INSPECTION — different problems
⚠️ Socat needs no SSH access — useful when SSH isn't available on the compromised host
