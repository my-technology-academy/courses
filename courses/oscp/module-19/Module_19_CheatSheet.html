EXAM TRAPS & ACRONYMS
⚠️ Metasploit does NOT replace manual technique — it automates specific pieces of it (M5/6, M10, M11, M15/16, M17)
⚠️ Staged payloads risk failure on poor/limited outbound connectivity — prefer non-staged there
⚠️ Every module's output (auxiliary, exploit, post-ex) is a candidate — verify manually before acting
⚠️ Generated executables still need a delivery route and still need evasion — nothing is automatic
⚠️ Script only PROVEN sequences — an unverified resource script automates a mistake repeatably
