
QUESTIONS:

Q1. A learner reads a technique, recognises it later in a write-up, but cannot reproduce it unaided. Which technique addresses this gap most directly?
    (A) Retrieval Practice
    (B) Re-reading the original module a second time
    (C) Increasing the number of hours studied per day
    (D) Switching to a visual-only learning style

Q2. Which pair of failures is correctly matched to its remedy?
    (A) Cognitive load is remedied by spaced practice; forgetting is remedied by breaking material into smaller parts
    (B) Forgetting is remedied by spaced retrieval; cognitive load is remedied by breaking material down and building fluency in parts
    (C) Both forgetting and cognitive load are remedied by re-reading the source material
    (D) Neither forgetting nor cognitive load can be remedied once they occur

Q3. What does the syllabus mean by a "Demonstrative Methodology"?
    (A) Material that supplies a correct procedure for the learner to follow exactly
    (B) Material that shows the reasoning behind an approach and requires the learner to extend it
    (C) A method of demonstrating tools to an audience during a live presentation
    (D) A grading method based on demonstrated exam scores

Q4. In the chmod -x chmod case study, what is the primary skill being exercised?
    (A) Memorising the exact sequence of commands that restores the permission
    (B) Expanding beyond the initial information set to find an approach the material never stated
    (C) Reading the official manual page for chmod in full
    (D) Reinstalling the operating system to reset default permissions

Q5. Which combination correctly matches a named technique to its mechanism?
    (A) The Leitner System schedules review using boxes, promoting cards on correct recall and demoting them on failure
    (B) The Feynman Technique requires flashcards organised into daily, weekly and monthly boxes
    (C) SQ3R is a six-step method that adds a Reflect step to PQ4R
    (D) Spaced Practice means concentrating all study of a topic into a single long session



ANSWER KEY (For Instructor Use)
==================================================
Q1: (A) Retrieval Practice
Q2: (B) Forgetting is remedied by spaced retrieval; cognitive load is remedied by breaking material down and building fluency in parts
Q3: (B) Material that shows the reasoning behind an approach and requires the learner to extend it
Q4: (B) Expanding beyond the initial information set to find an approach the material never stated
Q5: (A) The Leitner System schedules review using boxes, promoting cards on correct recall and demoting them on failure
