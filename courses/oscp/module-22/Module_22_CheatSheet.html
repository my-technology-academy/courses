22.2 ACTIVE DIRECTORY PERSISTENCE
Purpose: survive interruptions without repeating the full attack chain
Golden ticket: forged from domain key material — survives password resets
Shadow copy abuse: recovers material from a pre-remediation snapshot
RULE: remove ALL persistence at engagement end — professional/contractual obligation
EXAM TRAPS & ACRONYMS
⚠️ Pass the Hash (direct auth) ≠ Overpass the Hash (hash → Kerberos ticket) — different mechanisms
⚠️ PsExec is well-established = well-MONITORED — not always the best choice under detection focus
⚠️ Golden tickets are NOT removed by password resets — forged from domain key material itself
⚠️ Remediation isn't complete just because active malware is gone — check shadow copies too
⚠️ ALL persistence must be removed at engagement end, no exceptions
