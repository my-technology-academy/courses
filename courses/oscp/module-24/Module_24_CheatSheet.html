24.1 CHALLENGE LAB OVERVIEW
Different kinds exist: not one undifferentiated pool of machines
High-level overview first: orientation before entry, not a walkthrough
Mock labs: treat with EXAM-level discipline — genuine readiness evidence
24.2 CHALLENGE LAB DETAILS
Dependency: one machine/piece of info may gate access to another
IP ordering: means NOTHING — not a hint, not a sequence
Decoy machines: exist to waste time, not to be beaten
Routers/NAT: "unreachable" may just need the right pivot (M17)
Credentials: track & retry EVERYWHERE, don't discard after one use
24.3 THE OSCP EXAM
The final stated objective of the ENTIRE official syllabus. Every practical skill from Modules 1-23, and Module 3's exam-stress/readiness/approach guidance specifically, converges here.
EXAM TRAPS & ACRONYMS
⚠️ IP address order is NOT a hint about sequence, difficulty, or importance — a named, explicit misconception
⚠️ Not every machine is a required target — decoys exist specifically to cost you time
⚠️ A credential's usefulness isn't limited to the host it was found on — retry it everywhere
⚠️ "Unreachable" ≠ "out of scope" — map routers/NAT and consider a Module 17 pivot first
⚠️ Mock labs ARE readiness evidence when taken seriously — not casual practice
