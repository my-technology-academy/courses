The Takeaway

Four things to hold on to about how the answer reaches you.

  • 01Training builds the model. Inference uses it.Your conversation does not change the model's parameters — they are fixed between training runs.
  • 02The answer is generated, not looked up.The model predicts one token at a time from learned patterns. There is no stored answer and no fact-checking step.
  • 03The context window is the model's working memory.Anything outside it is simply absent, and no warning is given when material drops out.
  • 04Variation is by design; the cut-off is real.The same prompt can give different answers, and the base model knows nothing after its training cut-off. Verify anything time-sensitive.
